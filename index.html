Publications

SafeCine: Risk-Calibrated Physical Preference Supervision for Language-Conditioned Aerial Cinematography

Dongryeol Lee, Jaeyeon Bae, Taehwan Kim

8th Robot Learning Workshop at NeurIPS 2026, poster (non-archival)

OpenReview

Language-conditioned camera drones need to frame shots well without flying too close to people or obstacles. SafeCine aligns a diffusion proposal policy with physical safety using preferences mined from simulation: candidate trajectories are compared under matched physical perturbations, and only pairs that a 95% Wilson bound resolves are kept for DPO-style training, while ambiguous pairs are discarded.

Across eight scene families in Isaac Sim 4.5 / PhysX, worst-case candidate clearance (q0.05) rose from 0.391 m to 0.501 m. The paper also reports that this proposal-level gain did not carry over to the full closed-loop system, and it analyzes where that gap opens.

Research experience

IMML Lab, UNIST

Dec 2025 to present

Undergraduate research intern, advised by Prof. Taehwan Kim

  • RCL-CPM. Risk-Calibrated Local Counterfactual Preference Mining compares candidate trajectories under matched physical perturbations and keeps only pairs resolved by Wilson 95% bounds, for DPO-style alignment of a diffusion proposal policy for language-conditioned camera drones.
  • Isaac Sim benchmark. Closed-loop physical evaluation across 8 scene families in Isaac Sim 4.5 / PhysX. Candidate clearance q0.05 improved 0.391 m to 0.501 m, tested with parent-stratified bootstrap and exact McNemar tests.
  • What did not work. The proposal-level gain did not carry over to the full closed-loop system. This proposal-to-execution gap motivates my follow-up work.
