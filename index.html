About

I work on embodied AI that has to act in the physical world — aligning generative robot policies with physical safety, and understanding why gains measured on proposals do not always survive closed-loop execution.

I'm an undergraduate in Computer Science and Engineering (AI Track) at UNIST with a minor in Business Administration, and a research intern at the Interactive Multimodal Machine Learning Lab (IMML) advised by Prof. Taehwan Kim.

Research Experience

IMML Lab, UNIST — Undergraduate Research Intern
Advisor: Prof. Taehwan Kim · Dec 2025 – Present
  • RCL-CPM: Risk-Calibrated Local Counterfactual Preference Mining — compares candidate trajectories under matched physical perturbations and keeps only pairs resolved by Wilson 95% bounds, for DPO-style alignment of a diffusion proposal policy for language-conditioned camera drones.
  • Isaac Sim benchmark: Closed-loop physical evaluation across 8 scene families in Isaac Sim 4.5 / PhysX. Candidate clearance q0.05 improved 0.391 m → 0.501 m (+0.110 m), tested with parent-stratified bootstrap and exact McNemar tests.
  • Limitation: the proposal-level gain did not carry over to the full closed-loop system. This proposal–execution gap motivates ongoing follow-up work.
